Flying Speaker Notes

Your notes float above your call on a transparent background. A free Mac app shared with friends.

Download for Apple Silicon

macOS 14 or later · M1 or newer · Free · Manual updates

Install once

  1. Open the downloaded DMG and drag Flying Speaker Notes into Applications.
  2. Open the app from Applications. macOS may block it because this free build is not notarized by Apple. Close the warning.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, find Flying Speaker Notes and choose Open Anyway. Confirm opening this app.

Apple’s instructions. A Mac managed by your employer may prevent this exception.

Start with a note

Create a note, paste your text or import a local PDF. Choose “Im Overlay anzeigen” to show it in a floating window. ⌘F18 toggles the overlay; change the shortcut in Settings. The overlay may be visible when sharing your entire screen.

Connect Google Docs, optionally

Choose “Aus Google Docs importieren” → “Verbinden …” and sign in with your own Google account. Grant read access, paste a document link and choose “Importieren”.

This app is shared with a small circle of friends and is not verified by Google. Google may show an unverified-app warning during sign-in. Your organisation may block access.

Manual updates

Quit Flying Speaker Notes through its menu before replacing it with the latest download. Closing the editor window leaves the menu-bar app running. Your notes are stored separately and remain in place.

Your data

Notes and imported images are saved on your Mac. Linked Google Docs are checked directly with Google. Editing saves locally; writing changes back to Google Docs requires a separate explicit action and consent. Google credentials stay in your macOS Keychain. The app has no analytics or developer-operated server; it does not send your notes or Google credentials to Max. Remote images are downloaded directly from their source.

For a backup, copy this entire folder, including Images:

~/Library/Application Support/Flying Speaker Notes/
